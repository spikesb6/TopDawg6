Shapes

Short1–3 mm past the fingertip. Typing, training, everyday.
Medium3–6 mm. Elegant without getting in the way.
Long6 mm and up. Usually gel or Gel-X.

Gel-X by Aprés

Gel-X tips come in five shapes, from extra short to extra long. Natural tips are flatter and suit wider nail beds; Sculpted tips have a higher arch. Your tech chooses the one that fits.
