# Blockwild

A Minecraft-style block game that runs in the browser. Open `index.html`; it loads Three.js from cdnjs and needs no build step.

Mine, build and explore an endless world of plains, forests, deserts, snowy mountains and caves. On top of the classic loop it adds double jump, dash, a craftable glider, Jelly bounce blocks, Boost pads, lamps that light the area around them, chain-reaction TNT, Gloom slimes that come out at night, quests with rewards, pick and health upgrades, and a live minimap. It works with mouse and keyboard or touch controls, and saves your world in the browser.
