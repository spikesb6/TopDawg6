## Description

> Every night, the same question: what should we eat? Tap once and get one fun, good answer.
>
> **Surprise me** picks from everything. Or pick a vibe:
> - **Cook it**: easy recipes in 30 minutes or less, with a grocery list you can check off and share.
> - **Fast food**: quick, craveable spots near you that are open right now.
> - **Dine out**: sit-down places nearby, with directions in one tap.
> - **Delivery**: couch mode. Jump straight to DoorDash, Uber Eats or Grubhub.
>
> Filter by mood (comfy, fresh, spicy) and budget. Not feeling it? Spin again.
> Heart the ones you love, and Surprise me won't repeat anything you ate this week.
>
> No account needed. Sign in with Apple or Google to keep your faves on every phone.

## App Store privacy answers

Data linked to the user (only when signed in): **Contact info** (email, name), **Identifiers**
(user id), **User content** (faves and past picks). Used for app functionality.

Data not linked to the user: **Usage data** (product interaction, analytics), **Diagnostics**
(crash data, performance). Used for analytics and app functionality.

**Location**: coarse and precise location is used for app functionality only, not stored and
not linked to the user. No tracking across other companies' apps.

## Google Play data safety

Same as above: location (approximate and precise, not stored, for app functionality), personal
info (name, email, only with an account), app activity (in-app actions), app info and
performance (crash logs). Data is encrypted in transit. Users can request deletion in the app.
