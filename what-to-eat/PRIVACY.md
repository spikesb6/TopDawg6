"What to eat?" ("the app") is made by [your name or company] ("we"). This policy explains what
the app collects and why.

## Without an account

You can use the app without signing in. Your faves, past picks and grocery list are saved only
on your phone. Delete the app and they're gone.

## With an account

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we store:

- your account id, and the email address and name Apple or Google share with us;
- your faves (ideas and the Google name and id of places you heart);
- your past picks (what you tapped "Let's eat this" on, and when).

This lets your faves and past picks follow you to another phone. It's stored with Supabase,
our database provider. Only you can read your own data.

You can delete your account at any time in the app: **Faves > Delete account**. That removes
your account, faves and past picks from our servers right away.

## Location

When you choose "Use my location" on Fast food or Dine out, the app sends your approximate
position to our server to find places that are open near you. We don't store your location.
To save on costs, the list of nearby places is kept for 15 minutes for a rounded area of about
100 m, not tied to you. If you type a city instead, we use that the same way.

Place results come from Google Places. Your position is sent to Google to run the search.

## Delivery and maps

The DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub and Get directions buttons open those apps or websites with
the dish or place filled in. What happens there is covered by their own privacy policies.

## Analytics and crash reports

We use PostHog to see which parts of the app people use (for example, how often Surprise me is
tapped) and Sentry to find and fix crashes. Both receive an anonymous id, your device type and
app version. They never receive your name, email, location, or the names of places.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
