The loop

Survive waves. Hostiles push in from the edges of the compound. Every 5th wave brings a Juggernaut. HVT Commanders (gold diamond) carry guaranteed Epic+ loot.

Chain kills. Every kill without going down adds to your streak. At 3, 6, 9, 13 and 18 kills you earn UAV, Airstrike, Sentry Gun, Overdrive and Hellstorm. Every 5 kills you pick one of three Field Upgrades that stay on your gun for the run. Longer streaks also boost loot luck.

Loot everything. Enemies drop ammo, plates and gear. Hold E on supply crates. After each wave a supply drop lands under red smoke. Better-rarity attachments auto-equip; the rest go in your bag (Tab).

Keep what you find. All attachments you pick up are secured to your Armory when the run ends. Any gun you find unlocks that weapon and its rarity blueprint. Salvage upgrades attachment and weapon rarity.

Challenges unlock new weapons and pay out gear. Weapon kills level up mastery (bonus damage) and unlock camos.

Health regenerates after a few seconds out of fire. Armor plates soak damage first. Going down uses a self-revive kit and ends your streak. With no kits left, the run ends.
