Accepted features

  • Ground movement, sprint, jump, variable jump height
  • Free flight, boost flight, ascend/descend
  • Ground dash, air dash, pursuit dash
  • Air recovery and ground recovery
  • Lock-on camera with off-axis melee framing
  • Camera collision, occlusion, trauma shake
  • Frame-data melee: 4-link chain, heavy, charged heavy, launcher
  • Aerial chain, ground bounce, wall splat, hitstop
  • Guard, guard break, perfect guard, dodge, Phase Break
  • Super armor as a character trait
  • Energy, charging, cooldowns, refusal feedback
  • All 10 abilities + both transformations + both ultimates
  • Veyron AI: 18 tactics, 4 difficulty tiers, pattern adaptation
  • Procedural Ruins of Veyra with reactive energy barrier
  • Destructible monoliths, dust, embers
  • Pooled VFX, procedural rigs, synthesised audio
  • Full HUD, pause, restart, victory and defeat
  • Match flow: intro, KO, timeout, draw

Rejected and fixed this cycle

  • P0 Fighters had no body collision — targets sat inside the hitbox blind spot and combat silently stopped working
  • P0 Ultimate cinematic motes dealt damage — fake 22-hit combos and a 24–0 balance blowout
  • P0 Frame-keyed ability effects re-fired during hitstop — the ultimate deadlocked the match permanently
  • P1 Camera framing — Veyron fully eclipsed Kairo at melee range (rubric score 4 → 7)
  • P1 Knockdown could be looped OTG
  • P1 AI could never chain-cancel — longest combo was 2 hits
  • P1 Balance sweeps reused hardcoded AI seeds, so every match was identical
  • P1 AI stuck-detector fired during match intro and KO, when the sim deliberately isn't advancing

Known blockers

  • P0 (release) 60 fps on GPU hardware is unverified — not verifiable in this container
  • P1 Practical combo ceiling is 4; the systems support far longer routes but nothing rewards them yet
  • P1 No post-processing (bloom/radial blur) — the largest remaining spectacle gap
  • P2 Veyron's black warplate can lose its silhouette against unlit ground
  • P2 Tapped heavy resolves on release, costing one frame vs a light
