Know your brands

How to ask for each shade

Some brands print a shade number and some go by name only. Here's what to say at the front desk.

Gel-X by Aprés

Ask for the tip shape, then the length

Gel-X tips come in five shapes and run from extra short to extra long. Not every shape comes in every length.

Natural tips are flatter and suit flatter, wider nail beds. Sculpted tips have a higher arch and a deeper curve. Your tech will pick the one that fits your nails.

About the codes. OPI codes are Nail Lacquer numbers (for example NL S86). DND codes are the number on the cap, and DND duos come as a matching gel and regular polish. Aprés codes are gel shade numbers. CHANEL, Dior, Hermès and Gucci print a number on the bottle. Other brands are asked for by shade name. Codes were checked against retailer listings, and the wider catalog comes from the "All Nail Colors By Brand" guide (October 2026).

Screen colors are approximate. Check the bottle or the salon's swatch ring before your tech starts, since shades can be discontinued or out of stock. Mani Match is an independent guide and is not affiliated with any brand listed.
